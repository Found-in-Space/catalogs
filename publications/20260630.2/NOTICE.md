# Notice

This publication combines Found in Space original organization and derived
pair-context measurements with facts obtained from third-party astronomical
catalogues and services.

## Publication boundary

The Parquet product records possible Gaia DR3–Hipparcos pairings. H2BN
membership means that Gaia DR3 publishes the corresponding
`hipparcos2_best_neighbour` mapping. Local-scan membership means that the pair
falls within the recorded angular acquisition radius. Neither flag asserts
duplicate identity, selects a winner, recommends removal, or defines a merged
star record.

## Upstream material

The evidence refers to or derives measurements from:

- the ESA Gaia mission and Gaia Data Release 3;
- Gaia DR3 `hipparcos2_best_neighbour` and
  `hipparcos2_neighbourhood`;
- the Hipparcos new reduction, catalogue I/311/hip2;
- CDS/VizieR distribution and catalogue metadata; and
- the controlled Found in Space Gaia acquisition identified in `manifest.toml`.

Upstream data rights, acknowledgements, and terms are not replaced by the
Found in Space licence. Users redistributing or publishing results should
review the current Gaia, ESA, CDS/VizieR, and source-catalogue requirements.

## Omitted payloads

The raw Gaia VOTables, compact Gaia match table, raw Hipparcos ECSV, raw H2BN
ECSV, and Hipparcos2 neighbourhood ECSV are support inputs and are not
republished as payloads here. Their identities and hashes are recorded in
`evidence/support_input_provenance.json`.
