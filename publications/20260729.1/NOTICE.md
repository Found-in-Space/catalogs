# Notice for Publication 20260729.1

Publication: Optional solar reference
Release: `20260729.1`

## Scope

This publication contains one optional reference entry for the Sun. It does not
republish a Gaia, Hipparcos, or other survey-catalog row. The Sun is outside the
scope of the core Found in Space external-star catalog and is not automatically
included in a Found in Space octree.

The `(0, 0, 0) pc` placement is explicitly synthetic. It is an optional
application convention for a solar-origin scene, not measured astrometry.

## Scientific Sources

The Johnson V vegamag absolute magnitude is attributed to Willmer (2018). The
nominal effective temperature is attributed to IAU 2015 Resolution B3 and Prsa
et al. (2016). See `REFERENCES.md` for full citations and persistent links.

This publication records small factual values and source attribution. It does
not reproduce the papers, their tables, figures, or substantial copyrighted
text. Rights in the cited publications remain with their respective rights
holders.

## Interpretation

The IAU nominal effective temperature is exact as a conversion constant by
definition. It should not be interpreted as an uncertainty-free measurement of
the instantaneous physical Sun.

The absolute magnitude is passband- and magnitude-system-specific. Consumers
must not silently reinterpret the Johnson V vegamag value as bolometric
magnitude, Gaia G, or another filter.
