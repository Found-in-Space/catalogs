Source: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio,
**Deep Star Maps 2020**. Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA).
Gaia DR2 credit: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. The source also incorporates the other catalogs
described on [NASA's source page](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/).

Found in Space converted the celestial 4k EXR into a main map and two gnomonic
polar caps. The caps were resampled in linear RGB from the EXR and encoded
losslessly after display conversion. The separate constellation figures,
labels, coordinate grids and galactic projection are not used.

The source and [SVS usage policy](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/) were reviewed
on 2026-10-17. SVS describes content as public domain except where noted;
no separate restriction was identified for this specific EXR. Preserve NASA
