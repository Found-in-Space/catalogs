# Credits and source terms

Source image: **Deep Star Maps 2020**, NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center
Scientific Visualization Studio. Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA).
Star data: Gaia DR2, ESA/Gaia/DPAC.

This product converts only the celestial 4096 × 2048 OpenEXR map into a WebP
display texture. It excludes the separately supplied constellation figures,
labels, coordinate grids, and galactic projection. Found in Space performed
the conversion; NASA and ESA have not reviewed or endorsed this product.

The [source page](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/) and
[SVS policy](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/) were reviewed on 2026-10-17.
SVS describes content as public domain except where noted; no separate
copyright restriction was identified for this specific EXR. Retain these
credits when redistributing it. See [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) for scope and
[NASA's general guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).

The conversion uses Three.js 0.170.0 (MIT) as a build tool and FFmpeg/libwebp
as an external encoder. Their implementations are not included in the image.
