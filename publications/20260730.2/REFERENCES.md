# References for Publication 20260730.2

Publication: Found in Space stellar overrides
Release: `20260730.2`

Every executable row records a selected distance reference and notes. The
three retained Alpha Centauri records additionally identify their position,
photometry, and temperature sources. The 48 reviewed additions record
astrometry, photometry, and temperature donor rows and use the distance-source
groups below.

## Retained Alpha Centauri Overrides

- van Leeuwen, F. (2007), "Validation of the new Hipparcos reduction,"
  *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 474, 653–664; VizieR I/311,
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20078357>. Source astrometry for the
  HIP-targeted records and the propagated Alpha Cen A J2016.0 position.
- Kervella, P., Mignard, F., Mérand, A., and Thévenin, F. (2016), "Close
  stellar conjunctions of α Centauri A and B until 2050," *Astronomy &
  Astrophysics* 594, A107,
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629201>. Source of the
  `747.17 ± 0.61 mas` system parallax and the Alpha Cen AB visual-orbit
  context.
- Kervella, P., Bigot, L., Gallenne, A., and Thévenin, F. (2017), "The radii
  and limb darkenings of Alpha Centauri A and B," *Astronomy & Astrophysics*
  597, A137, <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629505>. Independent use
  of the same system parallax and fundamental-parameter cross-check.
- Heiter, U., Jofré, P., Gustafsson, B., et al. (2015), "Gaia FGK benchmark
  stars: Effective temperatures and surface gravities," *Astronomy &
  Astrophysics* 582, A49,
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201526319>. Source of the retained
  `5792 ± 16 K` and `5231 ± 20 K` temperatures.
- Ducati, J. R. (2002), "Catalogue of Stellar Photometry in Johnson's
  11-color system," VizieR II/237, bibcode `2002yCat.2237....0D`. SIMBAD
  serves Johnson V `0.01` for Alpha Cen A and `1.33` for Alpha Cen B with
  quality flag C.
- Jao, W.-C., Henry, T. J., Subasavage, J. P., et al. (2014), *Astronomical
  Journal* 147, 21, bibcode `2014AJ....147...21J`,
  <https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-6256/147/1/21>. SIMBAD source record for the
  representative Proxima Johnson V value `11.13`, quality D.
- Ribas, I., Gregg, M. D., Boyajian, T. S., and Bolmont, E. (2017), "The full
  spectral radiative properties of Proxima Centauri," *Astronomy &
  Astrophysics* 603, A58,
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201730582>. Source of the retained
  `2980 ± 80 K` effective temperature.
- Gaia Collaboration (2023), "Gaia Data Release 3: Summary of the content and
  survey properties," *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 674, A1,
  <https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243940>. Source catalog context for
  Proxima Gaia DR3 `5853498713190525696`, its J2016.0 coordinates, and
  `768.066539 ± 0.049872905 mas` parallax.
- SIMBAD astronomical database and VizieR catalog access tool, CDS,
  Strasbourg. Object-level identifiers, photometry values, quality flags, and
  bibliographic links were checked on 2026-07-30.
- Gaia DR3 `gaiadr3.hipparcos2_best_neighbour`, controlled artifact SHA-256
  `2590acdbfd6016527dcb028a76a4ee9ea7775e6c3161924f2a9844b1ce221159`.
  The three-target extraction is published in
  `evidence/alpha_cen_pairing_review.json`.
